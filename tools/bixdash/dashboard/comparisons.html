Reading these plots

Higher scores and lower budgets are better. Filled circles are final scores. Hollow circles are intermediate scores, when enabled.

Hover or focus a point for its run name and exact values. Complete final results are shown by default. Enable incomplete budgets to include recorded minimums, identified in the tooltip. Use the Plot checkboxes below to hide individual runs.

Points do not imply a learning curve. No intermediate scores are invented from the final result, and no line connects separate experiments.
